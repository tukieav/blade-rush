# Blade Rush desktop arena — vision QA

Reference bar: leading Knife Hit-style arcade clones use a single, instantly
legible throw action, dramatic target scale, and constant peripheral progress.
The review checks a desktop arena broadcast against that bar, rather than a
portrait game merely centred in a wide browser.

## Round 1 — 1280×720 composition

Observed the first full-arena capture after a forced clear. The live crowd,
spotlights, sponsor boards, boss card, loadout card, and reflective floor fill
both wings; no edge is an unused black band. The capture exposed intentional
clear-toast copy overlapping the target, so settled screenshots were delayed
past the clear celebration.

## Round 2 — onboarding hierarchy

Reviewed the settled 1280×720 frame. The mission card initially occupied the
target's top edge. It was moved to the lower action lane, preserving the score,
level, target silhouette, and first-hit spectacle as distinct visual layers.
Level 1–2 targets are 172px logical radius and each fresh run shows the direct
tap/Space cue; the first successful hit adds a larger spark burst, flash, and
toast.

## Round 3 — final responsiveness

Reviewed fresh captures at 1280×720, 1920×1080, and 390×844. Desktop maintains
a full-height centre lane with active arena wings; portrait retains the same
mechanics and a full viewport canvas. The smallest menu/back target is 46.2px
at 390×844 (greater than the 44px requirement). Automated pixel checks confirm
all tested edges are rendered and all runs completed with zero page errors.
